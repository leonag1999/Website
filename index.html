About

The story behind the stories

GL

Guillermo Leon is a writer and photographer whose work explores the intersections of memory, place, and identity. Born with a deep curiosity for the stories that live in quiet corners, he has spent years honing a craft that moves fluidly between prose, poetry, and the visual image.

His writing has been shaped by years of travel, observation, and a commitment to capturing the textures of lived experience. Whether through the lens of a camera or the precision of language, Guillermo seeks to illuminate what often goes unseen.

When not writing, he can be found wandering with a camera, searching for the light that reveals a place's true character.

Based in New York
